Om Nom Connect Classic

❤ ❤ ❤ ❤ ❤ (0 ratings)

Game Description

Om Nom and his friends need your help in his new colorful Onet Connect adventure! Help Om Nom to bring together his friends and share his tasty candies with them. In order to achieve this, you have to match the same cute monsters or candies with each other to make them disappear from the board. But the monsters and candies block each other from being matched, so you have to watch out and find the correct order and path to combine them. But don't worry, Om Nom has some great power-ups up his sleeve to help you out in difficult situations!

How to Play

  • Match same monsters or candies to make them disappear
  • Find the correct path between matching items
  • Watch out for blocking items between matches
  • Use power-ups to help in difficult situations
  • Clear the board to advance to next level
  • Try to achieve the highest score possible
  • Share candies with all of Om Nom's friends

Game Features

  • Classic Onet Connect gameplay
  • Colorful and cute graphics
  • Sweet candy matching mechanics
  • Helpful power-ups system
  • Magnifying glass for finding matches
  • Om Nom Shuffle feature
  • Suitable for all ages

Tips & Strategies

  • Plan your moves carefully to avoid blocking paths
  • Use power-ups strategically in difficult situations
  • Look for multiple matches before making a move
  • Clear corners first to create more matching opportunities
  • Save power-ups for later levels
  • Watch for special candy combinations
  • Practice to improve your matching speed
